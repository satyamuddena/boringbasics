What changed vs. today, and why

  1. 1Menu button grows to 44×44 (was 40×40) — it's the only way into every other admin page, right next to the notch.
  2. 2Tabs get a full 44px tap row, plus a fade + scroll-snap so "more tabs off-screen" reads as a swipeable row, not a dead end.
  3. 3On a card that needs a reply, WhatsApp becomes a full-width 50px button — today it's a ~28px chip, the smallest target for the most urgent action.
  4. 4The whole card opens the detail sheet (chevron signals it) instead of a tiny "View" label — one of three ~28px buttons crowded into a row.
  5. 5The centered pop-up becomes a bottom sheet on phones: drag handle, 44×44 close button pinned top-right, action buttons sized the same 44–52px as the list.
